Trinity SMP

Three Effects. Power Is Not Free.
A season-based competitive Minecraft server designed for content creators, featuring a modified Hardcore experience with strategic depth and high-risk gameplay.

🎯 Project Overview

Trinity SMP is a custom-designed Minecraft series server intended for content creators. The core concept is a modified Hardcore experience, offering high-risk gameplay with strategic depth, avoiding the repetitive nature of vanilla Hardcore.

⚡ Core Gameplay

  • Each player receives one unique random effect at season start
  • Effects are distributed only once per season
  • No reroll or replacement during active season

⚔️ PvP & Effect Transfer

  • Killing another player allows:
    • Stealing the target's effect, OR
    • Receiving effect voluntarily without killing
  • Maximum active effects per player: 3

Effect Loss Conditions

  • Environmental death (lava, fall, etc.)
  • Being killed by another player
  • Voluntary effect transfer

⚔️ Active Effect Mechanics

  • Effects that require a triggering action (for example: attacking)
  • Each attack has 1%–2.5% chance to apply an active effect
  • If triggered:
    • Effect Level: I
    • Duration: 3 seconds

✨ Passive Effect Rules

  • Activate immediately on acquisition
  • Effect Level: I
  • Duration: Infinite

Effect Balancing

  • Higher-level effects from items are ignored if a lower infinite passive effect exists
  • Maximum effect level remains capped
  • Applies to all similar overlapping effects

🩸 Custom Hardcore Mode

  • Enhanced Hardcore experience with unique mechanics
  • Custom resource pack for immersive health and status visuals
  • Death does not result in permanent elimination
  • Respawn delay: 3 hours
  • Designed to challenge players while allowing strategic recovery

🖥️ Server Configuration

  • Core mods: 2 Total
  • Additional plugins: None (Except Voice Chat)
  • Server RAM: 16 GB
  • Gameplay mode: Anarchy

Community Rule

Players may not watch videos or live streams from other participating creators.

📅 Season System

  • Effects distributed once at season start
  • Mid-season joins receive no effects
  • New effects appear only in next season

💰 Donation System

  • Players may donate any amount, anytime
  • First donation of $25 per season grants one random effect
  • Additional donations are support-only (no extra effects)
Note: Server is fully playable without donating. Donations fund server uptime and future seasons. Donors receive a one-time random effect identical to standard season effects.

🎨 Effect Visibility

  • 0–1 effect: Yellow indicator
  • 2 effects: Orange indicator
  • 3 effects: Red indicator
  • Effects can be stored as inventory items
  • Strategic effect removal before logout allowed

Anti-Instant Abuse

  • Effect items cannot be activated immediately after joining
  • Activation delay: 5 minutes per item
  • Removing active effects has 5-minute cooldown
  • Re-equipping effects has 1-minute delay
